Postern brand use policy
Version 1.0 — 2026-10-26

The Postern name, wordmark and door-shaped mark identify the Postern project
maintained by Chirayu (Kong) Wongchokprasitti through nerdymatters.

1. Scope

This policy concerns the Postern name and the distinctive Postern artwork in:

  - docs/brand/logo/wordmark/;
  - docs/brand/logo/icon/;
  - docs/brand/logo/favicon/ and their deployed favicon/app-icon copies;
  - the Postern wordmark outlines in dashboard/web/src/PosternWordmark.tsx.

It does not cover functional UI icons in docs/brand/logo/icons/ or
dashboard/web/src/PosternIcons.tsx, third-party icons or fonts, or source-code
identifiers. Their applicable software and third-party licenses remain intact.

The Postern artwork listed above is not offered under the project's MIT or
Apache-2.0 license. Permission to reproduce it is granted only as stated in
this policy or in separate written permission. This policy states the rights
the project grants to the extent it holds them; it does not claim trademark
registration or rights over unrelated uses of the word "postern."

2. Uses allowed without asking

You may:

  - refer accurately to Postern in documentation, reviews, tutorials and
    comparisons, using the name in ordinary text;
  - use an unaltered official logo to identify and link to the Postern project;
  - retain the supplied name and artwork when redistributing an unmodified
    official release or running it for your own organization;
  - describe an independent service as "Hosting for Postern," "Works with
    Postern," or "Powered by Postern," if the description is accurate and
    your own distinct service name is prominent;
  - reproduce screenshots of Postern in factual documentation and reporting.

An independent service must clearly identify its operator and must not imply
that it is operated, sponsored, certified or endorsed by Postern or nerdymatters.
An organization may configure its own training, notices, identity settings,
origins and operational settings without losing permission to retain the
supplied artwork in an otherwise unmodified release.

3. Uses requiring written permission

Do not use "Postern" or a confusingly similar name as the name of your own
product or hosted service, or use the Postern logo as your service's logo.
For example, "Postern Cloud," "Official Postern Hosting" and "Postern Pro"
require written permission. A descriptive phrase such as "Example Hosting
for Postern" is allowed under section 2.

Modified distributions must use a distinct product name and replace the
Postern wordmark, app icon and favicons. They may retain accurate statements
that they are based on Postern, along with required attribution notices.
Private modifications used only within your organization may retain the
supplied artwork if users are not misled about who operates the installation.

Do not alter the Postern logo, combine it into another mark, or register
confusing names, domains or social accounts without written permission.
Proportional resizing and the supplied light, dark and monochrome variants
are allowed. Do not imply endorsement or partnership that has not been granted.

4. Software rights and third-party rights

This policy adds no restriction to the MIT or Apache-2.0 license. You may use,
modify, distribute, sell and host the software, including a competing service,
under those licenses with your own branding. Preserve required copyright,
license and attribution notices when rebranding.

This policy does not restrict lawful nominative, descriptive or other uses
that do not require permission. It grants no rights in third-party marks,
including Google, NASA, Lucide, Feather or Outfit. The source-font license for
Outfit remains the SIL Open Font License; the Postern wordmark is resulting
artwork, not a font distribution.

For permission beyond this policy, contact the maintainer through
https://github.com/nerdymatters/postern. A public issue may be used for a
non-confidential brand-permission request. Permission must be in writing;
silence does not grant permission.
